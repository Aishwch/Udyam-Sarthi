# 🚀 Udyam Saarthi

### AI-Powered Digital Transformation Platform for MSMEs

## 📌 Project Overview

Udyam Saarthi is an AI-powered digital transformation platform designed to help Manufacturing MSMEs streamline business operations, optimize resources, improve regulatory compliance, and make data-driven decisions. The platform also promotes Industrial Symbiosis by connecting industries to utilize by-products as secondary raw materials and includes a Smart Scrap Marketplace to maximize scrap value through intelligent buyer recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Digitize MSME business operations.
- Optimize resource utilization using AI.
- Enable Industrial Symbiosis for sustainable manufacturing.
- Provide an intelligent Scrap Marketplace with market insights.
- Simplify compliance management and business monitoring.
- Support data-driven decision making through analytics dashboards.
